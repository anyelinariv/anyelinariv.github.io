    Survey questions: printable list

    The same ten questions as a plain list, with the answer key below. Use them to check what students learned.

    View all 10 questions and the answer key
    1. Multiple choice. Where is your bread plate?
      1. Upper left, above the forks
      2. Upper right, above the knife
      3. Directly above the dinner plate
      4. Wherever there is room
    2. Multiple choice. Soup is the first course. Which utensil do you use?
      1. The spoon farthest to the right
      2. The fork farthest to the left
      3. The spoon above the plate
      4. The utensil closest to the plate
    3. True or false. You should start eating as soon as your plate arrives so your food doesn't get cold.
    4. Multiple choice. When should you arrive at a business meal?
      1. Exactly on time
      2. 5–10 minutes early
      3. 30 minutes early
      4. A few minutes late, so the host is ready
    5. Multiple choice. Which is the best choice to order?
      1. The most expensive steak
      2. Spaghetti with tomato sauce
      3. A mid-priced chicken or fish dish
      4. Nothing, just a drink
    6. True or false. Keeping your phone face up on the table is fine if you're waiting for a call.
    7. Multiple choice. The salt is at the far end of the table. What do you do?
      1. Reach across to get it
      2. Stand up and walk over
      3. Ask the person nearest to pass it
      4. Do without
    8. Multiple choice. How do you show the server you have finished eating?
      1. Fork and knife side by side on the plate
      2. Fork and knife crossed on the plate
      3. Push the plate away
      4. Put your napkin on the plate
    9. True or false. According to Carreño, you may use the knife to bring food to your mouth once you've finished cutting.
    10. Short answer. When should you send a thank-you note after a business meal, and what should it mention?

    Answer key

    1. A. Upper left (B·M·W: bread, meal, water).
    2. A. Utensils are used from the outside in.
    3. False. Wait until everyone is served and the host begins.
    4. B. 5–10 minutes early.
    5. C. Mid-priced and easy to eat.
    6. False. Keep it silent and out of sight.
    7. C. Ask; never reach across someone.
    8. A. Side by side on the plate.
    9. False. The knife only cuts and serves.
    10. Within 24 hours; thank the host and mention something specific from the conversation.

    Bon appétit.
