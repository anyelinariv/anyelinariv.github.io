Before, during and after

The meal, step by step.

Seven moments where a good impression is made or lost, with what to do at each one.

  1. Before
  2. Arrival
  3. Ordering
  4. Eating
  5. Conversation
  6. Paying
  7. Thank-you
  1. 1

    Before the meal

    • RSVP within a day, and confirm time and place the day before.
    • Dress code: when unsure, go business casual or one step up.
    • Allergies or diet? Tell the host in advance, briefly.
    • Read the menu online and choose two options.
  2. 2

    Arrival

    • Arrive 5–10 minutes early. Late is never neutral.
    • Phone on silent and out of sight, in a pocket or bag, before you walk in.
    • Firm handshake, eye contact, and your full name.
    • Wait for the host to show you your seat before sitting.
  3. 3

    Ordering

    • Mid-priced and easy to eat: chicken, fish, salad.
    • Skip messy food: spaghetti, ribs, wings, big burgers.
    • Alcohol? Only if your host drinks, and only one.
    • Follow the host's lead on appetizers and dessert.
  4. 4

    Eating

    • Napkin on your lap right after you sit.
    • Wait until everyone is served and the host starts.
    • Small bites, so you can always answer a question.
    • Ask, don't reach. Pass salt and pepper together, to the right.
    Which fork? See the place setting →
  5. 5

    Conversation

    • Let the host start business talk, usually after ordering.
    • Talk to both neighbors, not only the recruiter.
    • Never talk with your mouth full.

    Easy openers

    "How did you get started at the company?" "What do you enjoy most about Tucson?" "What does a typical first year look like?"

    Leave off the table (topics to avoid)

    Politics & religion Salary (at a first meal) Complaints & gossip
  6. 6

    Paying

    • Whoever invites, pays. At a recruiter meal, that's the company.
    • Don't reach for the check. A sincere "thank you" is enough.
    • If you host: tip 18–20% in the U.S. and settle the bill discreetly.
  7. 7

    The thank-you

    • Stay a little after the meal; don't rush off.
    • Thank the host at the table.
    • Email within 24 hours. Mention one specific moment from the conversation.

    Subject: Thank you for lunch today

    Dear Ms. Rivera,

    Thank you for lunch and for explaining how your team tests new products. It made me even more excited about the internship.

    Best regards,
    Alex Smith
