Before, during and after

The meal, step by step.

Seven scenes. Tap a number, use the arrows or swipe.

Before

  • RSVP within a day
  • Check the dress code
  • Share allergies early
  • Preview the menu online

Arrival

  • 5–10 minutes early
  • Phone silent and away
  • Firm handshake, full name
  • Wait to be seated

Ordering

  • Mid-priced, easy to eat
  • Nothing messy
  • Alcohol: host first, max one
  • Follow the host's lead

Eating

  • Napkin on your lap
  • Wait for the host to start
  • Small bites
  • Ask, don't reach
Which fork? See the table →

Conversation

  • Host starts business talk
  • Talk to both neighbors
  • Never with a full mouth
Play: safe topic or skip it? ↓

Paying

  • Who invites, pays
  • Don't grab the check
  • Hosting? Tip 18–20%

Thank-you

  • Linger a little
  • Thank the host in person
  • Email within 24 hours
  • Mention one specific moment
