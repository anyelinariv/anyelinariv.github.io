Business Dining Etiquette: three design directions

Each preview shows the palette, the type pairing, and a mini home-page hero. Pick one or mix elements (for example, Option C colors with Option B illustrations).

Chosen: Option A, Maître d'. (Originally recommended: Option C.) It speaks directly to UA students, its two-color palette (one main color plus one accent) and grid match the graded design rules most cleanly, and its numbered "blueprint" diagram fits the interactive table-setting explorer.

A. Maître d'

Refined / classic restaurant
Mood
Candle-lit, elegant and calm, like a printed menu at a fine restaurant. Makes etiquette feel timeless and pairs well with the 1853 Carreño material.

Signature graphic
Engraved-style gold line-art place settings that draw themselves as you scroll (stroke animation).
