Think B · M · W

MMealcenter
WWaterright

Tonight's menu

Order it or skip it?

Browse the menu. Easy-to-eat dishes win.

✓ Order it

Grilled chicken

Easy to cut, easy to eat, sensibly priced.

✗ Skip it

Spaghetti

Twirling and sauce splashes steal the spotlight.

✓ Order it

Green salad

Light and fresh. Cut large leaves with your knife.

✗ Skip it

Whole lobster

Shells, tools and bibs. Save it for vacation.

✓ Order it

Salmon fillet

Flakes neatly with a fork and has few bones.

✗ Skip it

French onion soup

Strings of melted cheese and hot drips. Skip it.
