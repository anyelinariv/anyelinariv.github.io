The one trick to remember

Think B · M · W

Left to right, like the car brand: your bread, your meal, your water.

B Bread on your left
M Meal in the center
W Water on your right

Under the table, touch thumb to index finger on both hands: the left makes a b (bread), the right a d (drink).
